**Ghost** is a proactive AI assistant. You never prompt it. It watches the work
you're already doing, keeps track of the questions you left hanging, and tells you
when it sees something that answers one.

It speaks up only when it's confident and can show you what it saw. Everything else
waits in an inbox, and when there's nothing worth saying it stays quiet. It brings
you the finding and stops there. It never acts on your behalf.

All of it runs on your machine. Local Qwen model, local memory, nothing ever leaves
your disk. About 160k lines of C# so far.
